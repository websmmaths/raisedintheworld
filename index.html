About Ashley

Hi, I’m Ashley Thomas, founder of Raised in the World Travel. I believe travel can bring people closer, open up new perspectives, and create memories that stay with us long after we return home.

As a mom of three kids five and under, I know family travel can feel like a lot to manage. I help families find adventures that feel exciting and doable, with thoughtful planning and practical guidance to make the details easier.

I also plan travel for groups who want to share an experience together. That includes group cruises, celebrations and reunions, corporate and incentive travel, and trips for people with shared interests—like scuba diving, golf, or running. I can also help coordinate wedding room blocks and other group travel details, so everyone can focus on enjoying the experience.

Whether you’re planning a family adventure or bringing a group together, my goal is to help you travel with more confidence, enjoy the experience, and focus on the people you’re traveling with.
